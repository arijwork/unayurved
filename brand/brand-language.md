# Unayurved — Brand Language

*Established from independent market research. Date: 2026-09-21.*

---

## Positioning

**One sentence:** Unayurved makes cold-pressed oils and wellness products rooted in both Unani and Ayurvedic traditions, designed for people who want ancient remedies without ancient aesthetics.

**The gap we occupy:** Most Ayurvedic brands wrap their products in a visual language that signals "traditional" — earthy greens, gold, Sanskrit script, botanical illustrations, heritage imagery. This works for consumers who find comfort in tradition. But there is a growing segment — educated, urban, health-conscious — who believe in the efficacy of Ayurvedic and Unani formulations but find the packaging dated or inaccessible. No major Indian brand has credibly claimed the "modern Unani + Ayurvedic" space. That is our white space.

**What we are not:** We are not clinical (no pharmaceutical white + blue). We are not luxury-intimidating (no Forest Essentials pricing tier). We are not generic-wellness (no stock coconut illustrations, no "100% natural" banner as the primary message).

---

## Visual Direction

Three words that govern every design decision — and what they mean concretely:

**Editorial** — The label should look like it belongs on a curated shelf alongside a Kinfolk magazine. Generous white space. Strong typographic hierarchy. Information is presented, not shouted.

**Grounded** — Warmth over coolness. Cream over clinical white. The deep forest green (already in the logo) signals earth and plant origin without needing to show a leaf. No added botanical illustrations — the logo mark covers that register.

**Precise** — Every element has a reason to be where it is. Tight spacing on regulatory content. Clean table structures. No decorative elements that don't carry meaning.

## Color Palette

| Token | Hex | CMYK (approx.) | Use |
|-------|-----|-----------------|-----|
| `--color-bg` | `#FEFCF3` | C:0 M:1 Y:4 K:0 | Label background — warm white, like unbleached cotton |
| `--color-primary` | `#1C6B1C` | C:75 M:15 Y:100 K:20 | Logo green, headings, borders, key accents |
| `--color-text` | `#1A1A18` | C:0 M:0 Y:5 K:90 | All primary body text — near-black, not pure black |
| `--color-text-light` | `#5C5C52` | C:0 M:0 Y:10 K:64 | Secondary / regulatory text |
| `--color-divider` | `#C8BFA0` | C:0 M:5 Y:20 K:22 | Thin ruled lines, table borders — warm sand |
| `--color-accent` | `#FEFCF3` | — | Same as bg; no additional accent color — restraint is the point |

**What we deliberately avoided:**
- Gold (#B8860B and similar) — too associated with generic premium Ayurvedic
- Red — signals heat, medicine, danger
- Teal/turquoise — already used in Logo 2 for digital; keeping labels on the forest green

## Typography

All fonts are Google Fonts (free, no license required for commercial use on print).

| Role | Font | Weight | Use |
|------|------|--------|-----|
| Display | Cormorant Garamond | 500 Medium | Product name on front label — editorial, slightly heritage |
| Body | DM Sans | 400 Regular, 500 Medium | Claims, descriptions, back label prose |
| Regulatory | DM Sans | 400 Regular | Nutrition table, FSSAI block, dates, MRP — same family, just smaller |

**Why this pairing:** Cormorant Garamond at large sizes signals quality and care without being ornate. DM Sans is highly legible at 6–7pt, which matters for the back label. One serif + one geometric sans is the minimum premium system. No third typeface is introduced anywhere.

## Logo Treatment Rules

**Label-primary logo (Logo 1):** Full mark — 5-petal flower + UNAYURVED wordmark + "Unani | Ayurvedic" subtitle.
- Minimum size on label: 40mm wide (never smaller — the subtitle must remain legible)
- Clear space: minimum 4mm on all sides from any other element
- Acceptable backgrounds: `--color-bg` (warm white) only. Never placed on a colored background on this label.
- Do not recreate or redraw the wordmark as text — always use the logo PNG.

**Digital logo (Logo 2):** Reserved for website, social media, app — not used on print labels.

## Voice — What We Sound Like

Short. Confident. Not breathless. We don't over-explain.

**On the label:**
- "Virgin Coconut Oil" — not "Premium Extra Virgin Cold-Pressed Artisanal Coconut Oil"
- "Cold Pressed · Unrefined · Chemical Free" — three words, each earns its place
- "Rooted in Unani & Ayurvedic tradition" — one line, carries the brand story, needs nothing more

**We do not write:**
- Superlatives ("the finest," "the purest," "best in class")
- Fluffy wellness language ("nourishes your soul," "holistic harmony")
- Long ingredient stories on the label — that belongs on the website
