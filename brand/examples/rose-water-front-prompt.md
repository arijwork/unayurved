# Example: Pure Rose Water — Front Label Prompt

**Product:** Pure Rose Water, 200ml  
**Status:** Prompt written and refined in session. Not yet generated in Canva — this is the ready-to-paste prompt.  
**Note:** This is the second product in the line. It demonstrates how the template adapts: accent colour changes (green → dusty rose), botanical subject changes, processing claim changes. All fonts and layout zones stay fixed.

---

Create a premium product label illustration for a bottle of Pure Rose Water, 200ml. Portrait format, tall and narrow — same proportions as the coconut oil label (approximately 75mm wide × 115mm tall). Pure white background, smooth and clean.

**ILLUSTRATION ZONE — bottom 48% of the label, bleeds edge to edge:**

A richly detailed, photographic-realistic botanical scene. Several fully open Damask rose blooms (Rosa damascena) are the centrepiece — deep dusty rose-red (#C0485A range), not bright pink, not pale blush. The specific colour of traditional Indian Damask roses used for steam distillation. Petals are full, layered, slightly dewy, with natural depth and soft shadows. A few loose petals scattered at the base.

Around and behind the roses, dark glossy green rose foliage sweeps in from both sides and the bottom — the same compositional structure as the coconut oil label where palm fronds framed the coconuts. Leaves are rich, layered, with visible veins.

Two or three small unopened rosebuds appear at the edges. A few small clear droplets of water on petals suggest purity and distillation.

Bottom-left of the illustration in small bold white text: NET QTY: 200 ml
Bottom-right: small green square with green circle (Indian vegetarian symbol)

Style: photographic-realistic botanical illustration. Rich and tactile. Not watercolour, not flat.

---

**TEXT ZONE — top 52% of the label, pure white background:**

Leave the top 30% completely empty — this is reserved for the logo which will be placed manually.

Below the empty logo zone, add the following text elements in exact order, all horizontally centred:

**Element 1 — Processing claim line:**
A thin horizontal rule in dusty rose (#C0485A) — then the text — then another thin horizontal rule in dusty rose (#C0485A).
Text: STEAM DISTILLED
Font: Josefin Sans, Light weight, all uppercase
Colour: #A07878 (muted dusty rose-grey)
Size: very small — approximately 9pt
Letter spacing: very wide, approximately +250 tracking

**Element 2 — Product name, two lines:**
Line 1: ROSE
Line 2: WATER
Font: Playfair Display, Black weight (or the heaviest available Playfair Display). High-contrast thick-thin strokes, classic serif.
Colour: #1A1A18 (near-black, very slightly warm)
Size: very large — each word fills close to the full label width
Letter spacing: slightly negative, -10 to -20
Line spacing: 0.88 (tight, the two words sit close together)

**Element 3 — Descriptor lines:**
Line 1: Distilled from Damask Rose Petals
Line 2: 100% Pure  ·  Preservative Free
Font: DM Sans, Light weight
Colour: #999990 (light grey)
Size: very small — approximately 8pt
Letter spacing: normal
Line spacing: 1.5
Position: leave a clear gap below WATER before these lines appear, then another clear gap before the illustration begins

---

**Overall feel:** Minimal in the middle, all richness at the bottom. The product name ROSE / WATER is the visual hero. The top third is empty white. The label should feel like it has too much breathing room — that is intentional and correct.

---

## What to expect from Canva AI — fix checklist

- [ ] Logo zone should be empty — if Canva AI adds a placeholder, delete it. Logo goes in manually.
- [ ] Check for duplicate descriptor lines — Canva AI sometimes repeats lines from the illustration zone or adds a second copy below the product name
- [ ] Confirm illustration bleeds to all four edges — no white gap at sides or bottom
- [ ] NET QTY "200 ml" visible bottom-left in white
- [ ] Veg dot visible bottom-right
- [ ] Playfair Display Black applied to ROSE / WATER — check it didn't substitute a lighter weight
