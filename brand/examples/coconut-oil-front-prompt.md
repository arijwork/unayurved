# Example: Virgin Coconut Oil — Front Label Prompt

**Product:** Extra Virgin Coconut Oil, 500ml  
**Status:** Generated, printed, and approved. This is the reference prompt that produced the final design.  
**Canva design:** `DAHXzNXZGDY` (500ml working copy)

---

Create a premium product label for a 500ml bottle of virgin coconut oil. Portrait orientation, tall and narrow format (proportions roughly 2:3, width to height). The overall style is modern Ayurvedic luxury — clean, minimal, confident. Inspired by premium Indian wellness brands like Kapiva and Forest Essentials.

**Background:** Pure white, smooth and clean. No texture.

**Top zone (approximately top 30% of the label):**
Empty white space. The logo (five-petal flower mark + UNAYURVED wordmark + "Unani | Ayurvedic" tagline) will be placed here manually after generation. Do not generate any logo, badge, or placeholder text in this zone.

**Middle zone (approximately middle 22% of the label):**
Generous white space — this is breathing room, do not fill it. Centred content only.

First, a thin horizontal grey rule, then in very small uppercase grey sans-serif letters with wide tracking: **COLD PRESSED**. Then another thin rule.

Immediately below that, in very large, bold, dark charcoal serif typeface (Playfair Display Black — high contrast between thick and thin strokes): the word **EXTRA VIRGIN** on one line, then **COCONUT OIL** on the next line. These two lines are the dominant visual element of the entire label. They should be 3× the size of any other text on the label.
Font: Playfair Display, Black weight
Colour: #1A1A18 (near-black, slightly warm)
Size: very large — each line fills close to the full label width
Letter spacing: slightly negative, -10 to -20
Line spacing: 0.88

Below the product name, in very small, light grey sans-serif text (DM Sans Light), two lines centred:
Line 1: Made from fresh Indian coconuts
Line 2: Unrefined · Chemical Free
Colour: #999990
Size: approximately 8pt
Line spacing: 1.5

**Bottom zone (approximately bottom 48% of the label):**
A lush, illustrated botanical scene that fills edge to edge, bleeds to all sides. NOT photographic — a high-quality flat or semi-realistic illustration with painterly quality.

Two large tropical palm leaves (dark green, glossy, with visible midrib and leaflets) sweep in dramatically from the left and right sides, curving inward and framing the centre. Behind them, more layered palm fronds in slightly darker green for depth.

In the centre foreground, resting among the palm leaves, two halved coconuts showing the white flesh inside. The coconut shells are dark brown and fibrous. The cut face shows creamy white interior with a small dark centre eye. The coconuts are slightly tilted, one facing slightly left, one slightly right, like they have been freshly split.

The overall palette of this illustration zone is: deep forest green (#1A6B1A range), slightly lighter mid-greens, dark woody brown (#6B3010 range), and the bright white of coconut flesh.

In the very bottom left corner of the illustration, in small white bold text: **NET QTY: 500 ml**

In the very bottom right corner: small green square with green circle inside (Indian vegetarian dot symbol).

Style: photographic-realistic botanical illustration. Rich and tactile. Not watercolour, not flat.
