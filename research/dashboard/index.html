What these figures assume
Your costs
Liquid cost per litre, and bottle, cap and label for the 100 ml, 200 ml and 500 ml bottle. The 500 ml bottle is filled with 400 ml. All were given including GST; because you are GST-registered, the GST inside them is treated as claimed back.
Assumed by me
₹10 per order for the outer box and packing material. GST on the sale at 5% for the oils and 18% for rose water. No advertising, no returns or breakage, and no cost for your own packing time.
Amazon fees
No referral fee, since Amazon charges none on oils and haircare priced up to ₹1,000. A closing fee of ₹3 up to ₹300, ₹24 up to ₹500 and ₹35 above that. Pickup and delivery at ₹55 a parcel, which assumes each plastic bottle weighs under 500 g once boxed; a packed 400 ml fill is close to that limit and would cost ₹75 if it goes over. These come from Amazon's public fee page and should be confirmed in Seller Central.
MRP and shop price
Your shop price is treated as the MRP printed on the label, and a product cannot be listed above its MRP. Keep: the printed MRP already leaves room for the Amazon price. Raise: the printed MRP is below what the size sells for on Amazon. New: no label yet, so the MRP is set about 25% above the Amazon price.
Distributor
Shown both ways, because "60% of MRP" can mean either: the distributor keeps 60% and you receive 40%, or you receive 60%. Each allows ₹2 a bottle for the bulk carton and has no Amazon fees. The volume table above uses the 40% reading.
Three-month plan
Monthly totals of 1,500, 4,500 and 10,000 bottles are a staging of your 10,000 goal, not a forecast. Amazon bottles are tied to what existing sellers move in each launch size; the rest is what distributors and shops would need to take. Profit is before ad spend, returns and your own time. Stock cost is the liquid, bottle, label and box for that month's bottles.
Decision
Launch: real demand at this size and a workable profit. Later: workable, but a smaller market or an open question. Hold: little demand at this size online, or no profit.
400 ml fill
Buyers compare it with 500 ml bottles, so it is priced about 20% below the 500 ml market price. Volume comparisons use the 500 ml size.

How products were sorted
Revenue
Helium 10's estimate for each individual listing over a month. The parent-level column is not used, because it repeats a whole product family's total on every size variant.
Repeat placements
The same listing appearing twice in one file, once as a sponsored result and once as an organic one. Counted once. Different pack sizes of one product are separate listings and all stay.
Core
The pure, single-ingredient product for personal or food use. Olive oil covers pomace, extra light and extra virgin cooking oil. Rose water includes edible gulab jal. Sesame oil includes cooking and massage oil.
Adjacent
The same ingredient in another form: blends and combos, capsules, face wash, juice, medicated oil, cooking spray, or a toner with added actives such as niacinamide or aloe vera.
Wrong audience
The right ingredient sold for another use: neem oil for plants and pest control, puja and lamp oils, pet products.
Wrong product
Does not contain the searched ingredient at all, or is one of the other six products showing up in the wrong file. In the second case it is counted once, in its own market.
How it was decided
By keyword rules on the listing title and Amazon category, with the top sellers in each file read by hand. Low-revenue listings were not individually checked.
